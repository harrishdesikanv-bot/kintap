<h1>KinTap Privacy Policy</h1>
<p><strong>Effective date:</strong> 7 November 2026</p>
<p>KinTap keeps everything on your phone: we do not collect, store, sell or share any of your personal information. This policy explains what the app uses on your device, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the KinTap Android app (package <code>com.kintap.phone</code>), version 1.0.0 and later.</p>
<h2>Summary</h2>
<ul><li><strong>No data leaves your phone because of KinTap.</strong> The app has no servers, no account, no sign-in and no internet features.</li><li><strong>No ads, no analytics, no tracking.</strong> KinTap contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software from any company.</li><li><strong>Contacts, photos and settings are stored only on your phone</strong>, inside KinTap's private storage that other apps cannot read.</li><li><strong>Calls go through your phone and mobile network as normal.</strong> KinTap does not record calls.</li><li><strong>You stay in control.</strong> A backup file is only created when you choose to, and only sent where you choose to send it.</li><li><strong>Uninstalling KinTap deletes everything it stored.</strong></li></ul>

<h2>What we do not do</h2>
<ul><li>We do not collect, receive or store any personal information on our own computers or servers. We have none for KinTap.</li><li>We do not sell, rent, trade or share your information with anyone.</li><li>We do not show ads or use advertising IDs.</li><li>We do not use analytics, crash reporting, fingerprinting or any third-party software that collects data.</li><li>We do not record calls or listen to them.</li><li>We do not read your messages, location, files or other apps.</li><li>We do not use Android's automatic cloud backup for KinTap's data: it is switched off in the app, so your contacts and photos are not copied to any cloud backup.</li><li>We do not use your information for profiling, automated decisions or to train any AI.</li></ul>
<h2>Backups you choose to make, and other services</h2>
<p><strong>Backups.</strong> In Family Setup → Backup, you can save your KinTap contacts, photos and emergency number as one file. Nothing is backed up unless you tap a button:</p>
<ul><li><strong>Save to Downloads</strong> puts the file in your phone's Downloads folder.</li><li><strong>Share</strong> opens WhatsApp or another app you pick. The file then goes wherever you send it, and that app's privacy policy applies.</li><li><strong>Restore</strong> reads a backup file you choose and adds its contacts to KinTap.</li></ul>
<p>The backup file is not encrypted. Anyone who gets it can read the names, numbers and photos inside, so keep it somewhere private.</p>

<h2>Keeping, deleting and protecting your information</h2>
<p><strong>How long it is kept.</strong> Contacts, photos and settings stay in KinTap until you delete them or uninstall the app. KinTap does not keep its own copy of your call log.</p>
<p><strong>How to delete it.</strong></p>
<ul><li>Delete one contact: Family Setup → Contacts → Delete. Its photo is deleted with it.</li><li>Delete everything: Android Settings → Apps → KinTap → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall KinTap.</li><li>Backup files you saved or shared are not deleted automatically; remove them from Downloads or wherever you sent them.</li></ul>
<p><strong>How it is protected.</strong></p>
<ul><li>Android keeps KinTap's storage private to the app; other apps cannot read it.</li><li>The Family Setup PIN is stored as a salted one-way code, and Family Setup locks for 30 seconds after 5 wrong tries.</li><li>Because nothing is sent over the internet, there is no server that could be breached.</li><li>Your phone's own screen lock protects KinTap's data if the phone is lost. We recommend setting one.</li></ul>
<h2>Children, your rights and international users</h2>
<p><strong>Children.</strong> KinTap is made for older adults and their families. It is not directed at children under 13, though a child may use a phone set up by their family. Because KinTap collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.</p>
<p><strong>Your rights.</strong> Privacy laws such as the EU and UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, and the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights to access, correct, delete and move your personal data. With KinTap you exercise these directly on your phone:</p>
<ul><li><strong>Access and correct:</strong> view and edit any contact in Family Setup → Contacts.</li><li><strong>Delete:</strong> delete a contact, clear the app's storage, or uninstall.</li><li><strong>Move (portability):</strong> use Backup to save a file and restore it on another phone.</li></ul>
<p>We hold no copy of your data, so we cannot see, change or delete it for you. You can still contact us with any question.</p>
<p><strong>International users.</strong> KinTap works the same in every country and transfers no data across borders.</p>
